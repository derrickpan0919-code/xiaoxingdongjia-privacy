Audio and speaking practice

Tap the speaker beside an example to play it. If you cannot hear it, check the device media volume and audio output. You can listen to a speaking prompt before recording starts. Recording requires microphone permission.

Restore your purchase

Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. From Progress & Settings, open Unlock All Content, then tap Restore Purchases.

Back up study progress

Choose Export JSON in Progress & Settings and save it to Files from the share sheet. To restore on a new device, choose Import Backup and open the saved file.

试听和口语录音

点击例句旁的扬声器可播放。若听不到，请检查设备的媒体音量和输出设备。口语练习可先听题，点开始录音后再计时。录音需要允许 App 使用麦克风。

恢复已购买内容

使用原来购买时的 Apple 账户，在「进度与设置」进入「解锁全部内容」，选择「恢复购买」。

导出学习进度

在「进度与设置」选择「导出 JSON」，通过系统分享面板存到「文件」。在新设备上可选择「导入恢复」并打开这份备份。
