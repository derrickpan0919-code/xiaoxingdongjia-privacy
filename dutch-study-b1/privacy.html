Privacy Policy for Dutch Study B1

Updated 26 November 2026

Study data

You can use the app without creating an account. Progress, settings, mistakes, answers and self-assessments are stored on your device. The app has no ads, analytics or tracking and does not send study data to the developer.

Microphone and recordings

The app uses the microphone when you choose to record a speaking exercise. Recordings are held on your device for playback during the exercise and are released when you leave it. They are never uploaded or included in study backups. You can revoke microphone permission in your device settings.

Purchases and backups

Apple processes in-app purchases through the App Store. The app uses StoreKit to check access and the developer cannot see your payment card details. When exporting a study backup, you choose where to save it or who to share it with. A recipient's privacy policy applies to that service.

Managing local data

In Progress & Settings, you can export and restore progress or clear speaking and writing self-assessments. Deleting the app removes its local data. Backups saved elsewhere can be deleted in their chosen location.

Contact

The developer is tongda pan. For privacy or data questions, write to tpan5913@gmail.com. If you contact the developer, your email address and message are used to respond.

荷语精研 B1 隐私政策

更新日期 2026 年 11 月 26 日

学习记录

使用 App 无需注册账号。学习进度、设置、错题、填写的答案和自检记录保存在本机。App 不含广告、分析或追踪功能，也不会把这些学习数据发送给开发者。

麦克风和录音

只有你选择开始口语录音时，App 才会使用麦克风。录音用于当次练习的本地回放，离开口语练习页后释放。录音不会上传，也不会导出到学习备份。你可在系统设置中撤回麦克风权限。

购买和备份

内购由 Apple App Store 处理。App 通过 StoreKit 确认是否已解锁；开发者无法看到你的银行卡资料。导出学习备份时，由你选择保存位置或分享对象，接收方按其服务的隐私政策处理备份。

管理本机数据

可在「进度与设置」导出、恢复学习记录，或清除口语及写作自检记录。卸载 App 会移除 App 本机数据，已另存的备份需在保存位置删除。

联系我们

开发者 tongda pan。隐私或数据相关问题可发至 tpan5913@gmail.com。如你主动联系，开发者会用你提供的邮件地址和内容回复。
