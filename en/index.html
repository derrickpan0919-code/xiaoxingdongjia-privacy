Little Doer Privacy Policy

Policy updated: 22 October 2026

Little Doer is for children and their parents or caregivers. We designed it to work without the developer collecting personal information through the app.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not automatically collect, send to the developer, or sell names, contact details, photos, locations, exact dates of birth, payment information, device or advertising identifiers, or analytics about usage. The app has no ads, third-party analytics, account system or AI chat. Children’s screens do not contain external links. Settings, purchases and privacy and support links are in the parent area, behind a parent check.

Information stored on your device

Your selected age group, theme, family tasks and reward text, reward star targets, daily reminder time, completed task count, stars, last day you reached your goal, and daily goal are stored on your device. The app does not send this information to the developer or share it with others. Please do not put your child’s name, phone number, address or other sensitive information in family tasks or rewards.

Daily reminders are optional. Your device asks permission and creates the reminders locally. You can turn them off in the parent area.

Apple purchases and restoring purchases

The optional Family Growth Pack is a one-time, non-consumable purchase. Apple handles transactions and payments. The App Store shows the price. We do not receive payment card numbers or billing addresses.

The app uses Apple’s StoreKit to read the product ID, transaction verification result and current purchase entitlement. This allows it to unlock or restore the pack and update access after a refund or revocation. The app saves the unlock status locally and does not send purchase information to our servers. It checks purchase records when it launches, returns to the foreground or sees a transaction change. If it cannot verify a purchase at the time, it keeps the last saved status.

A parent can choose Restore Purchases on the pack screen. Apple may ask the parent to verify their App Store account. The app does not see the account password. Apple’s handling of information is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Keeping and deleting information

Settings and progress are kept while you use the app. In the parent area you can delete family tasks and rewards, turn off reminders, or clear completed tasks, stars and progress. Clearing progress is permanent and leaves age, theme, daily goal, reminders, family tasks, rewards and purchase entitlement in place.

Deleting the app removes its local data. Your device backup settings control any copies in system backups. After reinstalling, you can restore valid purchases through Apple. Deleting the app or clearing progress does not delete Apple’s order records or request a refund. Apple manages orders and refunds.

Changes

If a future version adds accounts, cloud sync or another feature that collects information, we will update this policy first and seek parental consent where needed.

Contact and support

For privacy questions, feedback or technical support, email tpan5913@gmail.com. If you contact us, we receive your email address and message and use them to respond. Please don’t send sensitive information about your child. Use the same address to ask about privacy or request that we delete a support email.
