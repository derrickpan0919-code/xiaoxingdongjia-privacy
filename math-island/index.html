数学小岛隐私政策

English

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 24 日

学习数据

数学小岛无需注册。学习档案、设置、作答记录和进度保存在设备上，用来继续练习和显示学习情况。应用不会向开发者发送这些数据。应用内没有广告或第三方分析工具。

听题与购买

听题使用应用内音频或设备上的系统朗读。家长区可以购买和恢复可选的「数学侦探挑战」。Apple 处理付款和收据，应用通过 App Store 确认是否应解锁内容。开发者不会在应用内请求银行卡信息。

导出、删除及支持

家长可以在家长区手动导出当前档案摘要。只有在你通过系统分享选择发送时，摘要才会交给所选的应用或收件人。你可以在家长区删除档案，清除该档案在应用内的作答、进度和设置。

如需帮助或想查询、删除你发来的支持邮件，请联系 tpan5913@gmail.com。我们使用来信邮箱和邮件内容来处理你的请求。请勿在邮件中附上孩子的敏感资料。

Math Island Privacy Policy

Updated 24 November 2026

Learning data

Math Island works without an account. Profiles, settings, answers and progress are stored on your device so you can continue practising and see your progress. The app does not send that data to the developer. The app has no advertising or third-party analytics.

Spoken questions and purchases

The app plays bundled audio or uses your device’s speech synthesis to read questions aloud. A parent can buy or restore the optional Math Detective Challenges in the parent area. Apple handles payments and receipts, and the app checks with the App Store to determine whether to unlock the content. The developer does not ask for payment card details in the app.

Export, deletion and support

A parent can export a profile summary from the parent area. The summary goes to the app or recipient you select when you choose to send it through the system share menu. Deleting a profile in the parent area removes that profile’s answers, progress and settings from the app.

For help, or to ask about or delete a support email you sent us, contact tpan5913@gmail.com. We use your email address and message to handle your request. Please leave children’s sensitive information out of your message.
