Support

For help with CueBell, email tpan5913@gmail.com. Include your iOS version and what you were trying to do. Please omit private meeting links and access codes.

If the calendar or alarms were denied, open Settings > Apps > CueBell and check the relevant permission. For recent meeting changes, open CueBell and tap Refresh Calendar. Background updates run when the system allows them.

For purchases, open CueBell’s Settings and use Restore Purchases. Manage subscriptions through your Apple Account.

Privacy policy

Last updated November 26, 2026.

CueBell requires no account. The app has no advertising, third-party analytics, or server for calendar data. When you grant calendar access, it reads event time, title, participation status and meeting links on your device to set and update alarms. System alarms and Live Activities may show meeting titles on the lock screen.

Settings, custom alarms, scheduling information and reminder history stay in CueBell's local app storage. If you open a meeting link or make a call, the destination app, website or telephone provider handles that interaction under its own privacy policy. Apple processes App Store purchases and subscriptions.

You can withdraw calendar and alarm permission in iOS Settings, delete manual alarms in CueBell, and remove CueBell's local app data by deleting the app. Calendar entries in your calendar account and Apple's purchase records are managed by the services that hold them. If you contact support, we receive the message and use it to respond. To request deletion of a support message or ask about this policy, contact tpan5913@gmail.com.

CueBell 隐私与支持

更新日期为 2026 年 11 月 26 日。需要帮助或希望删除支持邮件，可联系 tpan5913@gmail.com。请勿在邮件中附上私密会议链接或密码。

CueBell 无需账号，应用内没有广告、第三方分析或日历数据服务器。授权后，CueBell 在设备上读取会议时间、标题、参加状态和入会链接，用来设置及更新闹钟。锁屏上的系统闹钟或实时活动可能显示会议标题。

设置、自建闹钟、闹钟安排和提醒记录保存在本机。主动打开入会链接或拨号时，由相应 App、网站或电话服务处理。订阅和买断由 Apple 处理。

可在系统设置撤回日历或闹钟权限，在 CueBell 删除自建闹钟，或删除 App 来移除 CueBell 的本地应用数据。系统日历条目与 Apple 购买记录由相应服务管理。联系支持后，我们会收到邮件并用它答复问题。

日程改期或取消后，可打开 CueBell 点「刷新日历」立即检查。后台刷新由系统安排。换手机或重装后，可在 CueBell 的设置页恢复购买。
